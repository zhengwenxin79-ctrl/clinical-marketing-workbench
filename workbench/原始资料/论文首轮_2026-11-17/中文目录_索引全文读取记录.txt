### 慢性心力衰竭患者易损期信息需求偏好的纵向质性研究

唐荣欣 张岩 任蕾元 刘艳萍 唐应丽 梁闪 刘新灿

中华全科医师杂志2026年 25卷 11期

DOI: 10.3760/cma.j.cn114798-20260521-00489

摘要

HTML PDF

目的 探讨慢性心力衰竭患者在易损期内不同阶段的信息需求偏好及动态变化，为制定分阶段健康信息支持策略提供依据。 方法 该研究为质性访谈。采用目的抽样法，选取2025年9月至2026年2月于河南省某三级甲等医院心血管内科随访的18例慢性心力衰竭患者作为研究对象。基于时机理论框架与易损期阶段划分，在出院前、出院后1个月、出院后3个月3个时间点对每例患者进行3次纵向半结构式访谈。访谈围绕各阶段信息需求的核心内容、获取途径与体验、形式偏好及影响因素等方向展开。采用解释现象学分析法整理、分析资料。 结果 18例患者中，年龄48~78岁，男性10例、女性8例；纽约心脏病协会（NYHA）心功能分级Ⅱ级10例、Ⅲ级8例。共提炼出3个主题、9个亚主题：①极早期：安全过渡性信息需求（风险识别信息、用药安全信息、自我监测技能信息）；②早期：生活化信息整合需求（具体生活环境相匹配的个性化指导信息需求、对权威、可靠决策信息需求、对形式适宜、易于理解的信息呈现方式的需求）；③晚期：发展性与支持性信息需求（疾病远期预后与个人社会功能重建的信息关注、连续性专业信息支持可及性需求、同伴经验与情感性信息需求）。 结论 慢性心力衰竭患者在易损期不同阶段的信息需求偏好呈现动态、递进的演变特征。

### 2015—2025年我国全科医学教育研究热点和趋势的可视化分析

虞莹 邱潆潆 郑雅杰 杨华

中华全科医师杂志2026年 25卷 11期

DOI: 10.3760/cma.j.cn114798-20260614-00561

摘要

HTML PDF

目的 分析我国近10年全科医学教育研究热点及其变化趋势。 方法 采用可视化文献分析方法，检索2015—2025年在中国知网、万方数据库、维普数据库、中华医学期刊全文数据库发表的全科医学教育相关中文文献，利用CiteSpace从发文量、发文作者与研究机构分布、研究热点分布与演进趋势进行可视化分析。 结果 研究共纳入文献828篇。发文量呈现随年份“先升后降再回升”的趋势。核心作者集中于北京、浙江、上海、广东等发达地区大学及其附属医院，作者合作网络密度与机构合作网络密度较低，分别为0.003 1、0.002 5。高频、高中心性关键词包括“全科医师”“人才培养”“住院医师规范化培训”“教学改革”等。早期研究主要集中在“启示”“农村”“助理全科医师”等方面，而“课程思政”“新医科”“互联网+”“临床能力”成为当前该领域的研究热点。 结论 我国全科医学教育研究主体呈现“发达地区集聚、院校主导”现象，且作者与机构间合作网络松散。研究热点的变化表明该领域已从初期的规模扩张逐步转向高质量的内涵式提升。

Secular trends in society-, school-, and individual-level factors related to physical activity and their associations with elevated blood pressure among 0.6 million children and adolescents

Jieyu Liu, Xinli Song, Qi Ma, Jianuo Jiang, Yi Zhang, Ruolin Wang, Ziqi Dong, Li Chen, Yang Qin, Wen Yuan 等

中华医学杂志英文版2026年 139卷 13期

DOI: 10.1097/CM9.0000000000003868

摘要

HTML

Background: Physical activity (PA) is a key modifiable factor for elevated blood pressure (EBP). We aimed to clarify the evolving relationships between PA and EBP in childhood or adolescence by examining multilevel factors within an ecological model. Methods: National data were extracted from 2010, 2014, and 2019 cycles of the Chinese National Survey on Students’ Constitution and Health, using a multistage stratified cluster sampling design. We defined EBP by the updated Chinese reference standards and obesity by sex- and age-specific body mass index (BMI) cutoffs. Inadequate PA was defined as <1 h/day; ecological factors at society-, school-, and individual-level were collected through a questionnaire. Conclusions: A rise in EBP burdens during 2010–2019 contrasted with an increase in PA. PA-related ecological factors across society-, school-, and individual-level exhibited complex interactions on EBP. Although the impact of individual-level lifestyles on EBP was waning and obesity remained a primary concern, there was increased recognition of societal- and school-level’s ecological factors related to PA during 2010–2019. Promoting PA requires a comprehensive approach that addresses both facilitating and inhibiting factors, which is crucial for healthy blood pressure (BP).

Background: Adjuvant chemotherapy is the standard management approach for patients with stage II/III colon cancer; however, the effectiveness in older patients is still unclear. This study aimed to explore the efficacy of adjuvant chemotherapy and whether oxaliplatin-based chemotherapy has better oncological outcomes than capecitabine monotherapy in older patients with stage III or high-risk stage II colon cancer. Methods: We analyzed and compared oxaliplatin-based adjuvant chemotherapy with capecitabine monotherapy and non-adjuvant chemotherapy in 468 patients aged ≥70 years with stage III and high-risk stage II colon cancer, and a propensity score-matched analysis was conducted. The endpoints were overall survival (OS), cancer-specific survival (CSS), local recurrence-free survival (LRFS), and distant metastasis-free survival (DMFS). Results: Multivariate analysis shows that adjuvant-treated stage III or high-risk stage II patients experienced a highly significant increase in OS (hazard ratio [HR]: 0.34, 95% confidence interval [CI]: 0.20–0.59, P <0.001) and CSS (HR: 0.39, 95% CI: 0.20–0.75, P = 0.005) compared with the control group without adjuvant chemotherapy. By contrast, no statistically significant difference was observed in either LRFS or DMFS (all P >0.05). The subgroup analysis suggested that no statistically significant benefits were observed between oxaliplatin-based adjuvant chemotherapy and capecitabine monotherapy, and no significant differences in oncological outcomes were detected with ≥6 months of therapy compared with 3–6 months (excluding 6 months) of therapy. Conclusion: Older patients with stage III or high-risk stage II colon cancer can benefit from adjuvant chemotherapy, and capecitabine monotherapy has non-inferior oncological outcomes compared with oxaliplatin-based regimens.

HTML

回顾性选择复旦大学附属华山医院2023年10月至2024年6月收治的明确诊断为细菌性脑膜炎（BM）的患者，在入院第1、7、14、30天留取脑脊液（CSF）和静脉血，检测免疫分子可溶性白细胞分化抗原14（sCD14）、可溶性B7同源物3（sB7-H3）和炎症因子基质金属蛋白酶-9（MMP-9）、白细胞介素-6（IL-6）水平，并结合其临床资料分析其动态变化。共纳入4例患者，女1例，男3例，年龄50（46，56）岁，经抗感染治疗后，临床症状均明显好转。4例患者CSF的 sCD14、sB7-H3、MMP-9和IL-6水平经治疗后均较前明显下降。其中，CSF免疫因子sCD14和sB7-H3水平在治疗后呈逐步下降的趋势，而CSF的炎性因子MMP-9，尤其是IL-6的水平一经治疗即快速下降。此外，血清中sCD14和sB7-H3水平在治疗前后无显著变化。相较于MMP-9和IL-6，开展CSF中sCD14和 sB7-H3的动态监测对BM的疗效评估可能更有参考价值。
