## XJTU-hosted journal solidifies position in top 4% globally

June 23, 2026

  L M S

Clarivate Analytics released the 2025 Journal Citation Reports (JCR) on June 17. The Journal of Pharmaceutical Analysis (JPA), hosted by Xi'an Jiaotong University (XJTU), achieved a latest impact factor of 11.2.

This cements its position at the forefront of the Q1 tier, ranking 15th out of 356 journals in the Pharmacology & Pharmacy category, putting it within the top 4 percent globally. Additionally, JPA secured a 2025 CiteScore of 12 in the Scopus database, marking its eighth consecutive year ranking first among 52 academic journals worldwide in the Health Professions: Pharmacy field.

Since its inception in 2011, JPA has been dedicated to providing a high-level academic exchange platform for researchers and professionals in pharmacy and related fields worldwide, growing into a pharmaceutical journal with significant international influence. The journal has established a strong global network of editorial board members and peer reviewers spanning more than 70 countries and regions.

JPA is currently indexed in 16 major domestic and international databases, including SCIE, PubMed, Scopus, and CSCD. It has also been selected for the "English Leading Journals Project" under Phase II of the China Action Plan for Excellence in Science and Technology Journals (one of only 50 journals nationwide) and has been named one of the "Most Internationally Influential Academic Journals of China" (top 5 percent) for eight consecutive years since 2018.--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

## XJTU's six journals recognized in 2025 top international influence list

December 18, 2025

  L M S

Image: 1767082117778093548.png

Journal of Pharmaceutical Analysis is named a "top Chinese academic journal with international influence".

The Annual Report for International Citation of Chinese Academic Journals (2025), jointly launched by CNKI and Tsinghua University Library on Dec 11, announced the "2025 Top Journals with International Influence" list.

The list evaluated journals using the comprehensive Journal International Citation Index (ICCI) and selected 330 "top Chinese academic journals with international influence" and 330 "excellent Chinese academic journals with international influence" across four categories: natural sciences and engineering (English and Chinese) and humanities and social sciences (English and Chinese).

Xi'an Jiaotong University (XJTU) had six journals recognized across three categories, including one "top Chinese academic journal with international influence" and five "excellent Chinese academic journals with international influence", marking a 50 percent increase from the previous year.

Journal of Pharmaceutical Analysis (English)‌ was named a "top Chinese academic journal with international influence" for the eighth consecutive year, achieving an international citation impact factor of 9.292, an ICCI index of 138.959, and over 20,000 overseas citing authors.

Data Science and Management (English)‌, ‌Energy Storage and Energy Conservation (English)‌, and ‌Journal of Advanced Dielectrics (English)‌ were recognized as "excellent Chinese academic journals with international influence". Data Science and Management (English) achieved an international citation impact factor of 8.283.

Journal of Applied Mechanics‌ and Contemporary Economic Science‌ were named "excellent Chinese academic journals with international influence" in the humanities and social sciences category (Chinese).
