简历：
Prof Jenkins is an endocrinologist and clinician-researcher specializing in diabetes and vascular medicine.
She currently leads the Diabetes and Vascular Medicine Laboratory at the Baker Heart and Diabetes Institute in Melbourne. Prof Jenkins has over 30 years of clinical and research experience, focusing on preventing complications from type 1 and type 2 diabetes and heart disease. Her work emphasizes integrating technology into healthcare, including insulin pumps, glucose sensors and telemedicine. She has led major clinical trials such as REMOVAL, FIELD, and FAME-1 Eye.
Prof Jenkins is also actively involved in initiatives to support the provision of diabetes care in emergency situations and was co-Chair of the IDF-WPR Diabetes Care and Disaster Guidelines.
